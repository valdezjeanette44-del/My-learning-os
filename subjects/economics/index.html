Economics studies how people and societies make choices when resources are limited.

Lesson 1: What is scarcity?

Scarcity means there are not enough resources to satisfy every possible want. Choosing one option usually means giving up another. Economists call the value of the next-best option given up the opportunity cost.

Think about this: If you spend an afternoon studying, what might be your opportunity cost?
