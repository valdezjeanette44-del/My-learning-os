Philosophy examines fundamental questions through clear concepts, careful arguments, and critical thinking.

Lesson 1: What is philosophy?

A philosophical argument gives reasons to support a conclusion. To evaluate it, identify the conclusion, examine its premises, and ask whether the reasons actually support what is being claimed.

Think about this: What makes a belief reasonable to hold?
