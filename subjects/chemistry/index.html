Chemistry studies the composition, structure, properties, and transformations of matter.

Lesson 1: What is matter made of?

Atoms are building blocks of ordinary matter. Atoms can join through chemical bonds to form molecules. In a chemical reaction, atoms are rearranged into new combinations; they are not simply created or destroyed.
